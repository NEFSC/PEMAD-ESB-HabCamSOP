Welcome to the official Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) manual for the HABitat CAMera (HabCam) system. HabCam is a state-of-the-art, non-invasive, towed underwater imaging vehicle equipped with high-resolution stereo cameras and oceanographic sensors, primarily used to conduct benthic habitat characterization and population assessments for species such as the Atlantic sea scallop. This comprehensive SOP serves as the master instructional manual, outlining the rigorous protocols required for all phases of HabCam operations—including hardware assembly, fiber optic terminations, software configuration, at-sea deployment, data acquisition, and routine maintenance. The HabCam platform is engineered, operated, and maintained by the Advanced Technology Group, which resides within the Marine Development & Advanced Technology (MDAT) program, part of the Ecosystems Surveys Branch (ESB) under the Population & Ecosystems Monitoring & Analysis Division (PEMAD) at the NOAA Fisheries (NMFS) Northeast Fisheries Science Center (NEFSC).
